How it works

From a few honest answers to a clear picture.

Choose what best describes a typical week. There are no “good” or “bad” people here — only habits you can work on.

1

Answer 13 quick questions

One at a time, covering food, hydration, movement, sleep and daily habits. Tap an answer, move on — it takes about three minutes.

2

We analyse your pattern

Your answers are scored on your device using a simple, transparent method. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored.

3

Get your personalised report

See a habit-support score, charts by area, what you’re doing well and practical next steps matched to your answers.

Why it matters

Small habits, compounding over years.

Public-health bodies consistently point to the same everyday foundations: how we eat, how much we move, how well we sleep, and what we avoid.

150min

Weekly moderate activity

The minimum WHO suggests for adults, alongside reducing long periods of sitting.

7–9hrs

A typical adult sleep range

Needs vary by person and age, but regular, sufficient sleep supports mood, focus and recovery.

<10%

Of energy from free sugars

WHO guidance for a healthy diet — sugary drinks are one of the easiest places to cut back.

Grp 1

Processed meat (IARC)

Classified as carcinogenic to humans. The label reflects strength of evidence, not the size of an individual’s risk.

Sources: WHO healthy diet and physical activity fact sheets, IARC/WHO on processed meat, and CDC sleep guidance. Primefood is an educational reflection tool, not medical advice.

Our approach

Built to inform, never to alarm.

Private by design

Your answers are processed entirely in this page. There is no account, no tracking of your responses and no server upload.

Educational, not diagnostic

Your score reflects habits you report. It is not a clinical risk assessment and cannot predict any disease.

Grounded in public guidance

Suggestions draw on widely used guidance from WHO, IARC and CDC — and always encourage professional advice when needed.
